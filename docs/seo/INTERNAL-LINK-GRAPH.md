## 2. Cross-Linking Rules
1. **Service &rarr; Sector**: Every service links to primary sectors where that maintenance capability is most critical.
2. **Sector &rarr; Service**: Every sector links to required core services and maintenance regimes.
3. **Geo &rarr; Local Sectors & Proof**: Location hubs link to regional industrial corridors, primary sectors, and verified regional case studies.
4. **Guides &rarr; Commercial Anchors**: Informational guides link into commercial service quote request pathways and interactive tools.
5. **Zero Orphans**: All 326 registered routes have multiple verified inlinks from directory hubs, breadcrumbs, related rails, and HTML/XML sitemaps.
