# EntireFM Citation Quality Standard & Editorial Baseline

## 1. Citation Criteria
To ensure our content is worthy of citation by journalists, industry researchers, and AI answer engines:
1. **Unambiguous Definitions**: Technical concepts must open with an authoritative 1–2 sentence statement of what the term is and why it matters.
2. **First-Party Data or Direct Primary Citations**: Quote direct UK statutes (e.g. *Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005 Article 9*) rather than third-party commentary.
3. **Structured Formats**: Use clean markdown/HTML tables with column scopes, numbered decision trees, and bulleted criteria lists.
4. **Contextual Operational Nuance**: Explain real engineering tradeoffs (e.g. *why predictive vibration sensors are ineffective on non-critical split AC units*).
