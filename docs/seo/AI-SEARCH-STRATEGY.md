# EntireFM AI Search & Answer Engine Strategy

## 1. Core Principles
1. **Answer-First Content**: Informational articles and guides answer the core question within the first 60–90 words before expanding into technical nuance.
2. **Citation-Ready Structure**: Clean semantic headings (`H2`, `H3`), structured HTML tables, decision frameworks, and bulleted lists that AI models can accurately extract.
3. **Canonical Topical Entity Graph**: Definitive separation between commercial service intent (`/ppm`), broad educational intent (`/resources/guides/ppm-guide`), and terminology definitions (`/facilities-management-glossary#ppm`).
4. **Transparent Sourcing**: Primary references directly citing UK Legislation (RRO 2005, Electricity at Work 1989), British Standards (BS 7671, BS 5266, BS 5839), and HSE Approved Codes of Practice (ACOP L8).
