# EntireFM Geo SEO Expansion Plan (Phase 2A Discovery)

## 1. 100-Point Priority Scoring Methodology
Every proposed service × location expansion candidate is evaluated against 7 objective criteria:
1. **Commercial Buyer Intent** (`/25`): Likelihood that searchers are high-value B2B facilities/property buyers with an active tender or operational emergency.
2. **Search Opportunity & Demand Profile** (`/20`): Relative market size and commercial search volume in that conurbation.
3. **Strategic Regional Importance** (`/15`): EntireFM operational coverage, engineer presence, and mobile capacity.
4. **Service Scope Strategic Fit** (`/15`): Core engineering / Hard FM / Compliance revenue vs commodity low-margin tasks.
5. **Local Content Differentiation Feasibility** (`/10`): Availability of genuine local building stock, regulations (e.g. ULEZ, listed buildings, heavy power), and district narratives.
6. **Internal Link Graph Strength** (`/10`): Ability to seamlessly weave into the city knowledge graph without orphaned status.
7. **Existing Domain Authority Relevance** (`/5`): Relevance to existing ranking URLs and company credentials.

### Score Classifications:
- **`80–100`**: **PRIORITY 1** — Recommended for immediate Phase 2B Batch 1 rollout.
- **`65–79`**: **PRIORITY 2** — Recommended for Phase 2B Batch 2 rollout.
- **`50–64`**: **PRIORITY 3** — Scheduled for Phase 2C.
- **`< 50`**: **HOLD / DO NOT CREATE** — Insufficient demand or differentiation.

## 4. Local Content Feasibility Verification
Every proposed Tier 1 page has distinct physical and operational building stock that will ensure 100% unique, defensible local content without templated duplicate copy:

- **London**: High-rise commercial towers (City of London, Canary Wharf, Southbank), ULEZ / Congestion charging access constraints, listed facade consent, dual electrical substations, tenant demise demarcation.
- **Manchester**: Mill and warehouse conversions with retrofitted services (Ancoats, NOMA), Grade A office BMS integration (Spinningfields), Europe's largest industrial estate (Trafford Park uptime), Metrolink pedestrian access.
- **Birmingham**: Colmore Business District listed stock, Birmingham Clean Air Zone fleet requirements, canal-side basement plant water table exposure, NEC fixed event deadlines.
- **Leeds**: Wellington Place institutional leases with strict contractual SLAs, Aire Valley shift logistics maintenance windows, south-bank conversion fire compartmentation.
- **Sheffield**: Advanced Manufacturing Park (AMP) cleanrooms and tight humidity tolerance, Lower Don Valley high-load three-phase electrical and LEV extraction, topographic lifting challenges.
- **Liverpool**: Waterfront salt-air accelerated corrosion regimes on external condensers, driving rain curtain walling ingress, continuous port freight schedules.
- **Nottingham**: Workplace Parking Levy budgeting, NET tram line access constraints, Lace Market listed risers, life-science validated environments.
- **Derby**: Aerospace / automotive production-driven shift shutdown windows, pressure systems and LEV statutory examination, Derwent Valley World Heritage constraints.

## 5. Internal Link Graph Architecture

```
                       /locations (National Hub)
                                   ↓
                       /locations/london (City Hub)
                         ↓                      ↓
     /locations/london/services (Directory)     /london-facilities-management
                         ↓                                  ↓
      ┌──────────────────┴──────────────────┐          /fm-london
      ↓                  ↓                  ↓               ↓
 /ppm-london        /hvac-london       /commercial-    /facilities-
                                     cleaning-london  management-london
      ↓                  ↓                  ↓
 /services/ppm      /services/hvac     /services/commercial-
                                            cleaning
```

### Knowledge Graph Traversal Rules:
1. **Vertical Hierarchy**: `/locations` $\rightarrow$ `/locations/[city]` $\rightarrow$ `/locations/[city]/services` $\rightarrow$ Specific Service Page (`/ppm-london`) $\rightarrow$ Pillar Service Page (`/ppm`).
2. **Cross-City Knowledge Links**: Every city page links to 5–6 adjacent conurbations along the same regional transport corridor (e.g. London links to Slough, Watford, Croydon; Manchester links to Liverpool, Leeds, Sheffield).
3. **Sector & Proof Anchors**: Every service location page cross-links to relevant sector landing pages (`/sectors/commercial-offices`, `/sectors/industrial-manufacturing`) and relevant verified case studies.
