# EntireFM Service × Location Opportunity Matrix (Phase 2A)

## Matrix Classification Key
- **`EXISTING`**: URL already exists in route estate and is protected. Do NOT duplicate or alter.
- **`MISSING-HIGH`**: High commercial intent + strong search opportunity + defensible local content. Recommended for Phase 2B Priority Batch.
- **`MISSING-MEDIUM`**: Medium commercial intent + viable regional relevance. Phase 2B Secondary Batch.
- **`MISSING-LOW`**: Niche intent or smaller market footprint. Hold for Phase 2C.
- **`DRAFT-ONLY`**: Informational / internal staging only.
- **`DO-NOT-CREATE`**: Unviable, zero commercial differentiation, programmatic spam risk, or cannibalises core capability.
