# EntireFM Trust Architecture & Proof Governance

## 1. Principles of Commercial Credibility
1. **Zero Synthetic Proof**: No fabricated savings percentages, invented client names, fake star reviews, or stock-photo "engineers" presented as real staff.
2. **Anonymised Operational Truth Over Vanity Logos**: Substantive descriptions of real technical challenges (e.g. AHU heat exchanger remedials, 3-hour emergency lighting discharge cycles) provide far higher commercial credibility to experienced FM buyers than unverified client logo walls.
3. **Canonical Verification Register**: All accreditations and client permissions are tracked inside `/admin/content/trust` with expiration dates and verification evidence.
