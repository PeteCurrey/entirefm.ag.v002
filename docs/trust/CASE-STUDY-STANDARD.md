# EntireFM Case Study & Project Standard

## 1. Case Study Editorial Structure
Every case study published on EntireFM.com must follow this factual narrative:
1. **The Estate & Operational Context**: Building type, square footage / layout profile, and operational constraints (e.g. trading hours, out-of-hours access).
2. **The Operational Challenge**: What engineering or management failure existed (e.g. disjointed contractors, lack of statutory asset hierarchy, recurrent HVAC tripping).
3. **The Technical Approach**: What EntireFM physically performed (e.g. Uniclass 2015 asset logging, CIBSE Guide M scheduled testing, primary chiller compressor overhaul).
4. **The Verifiable Outcome**: Factual operational improvements (e.g. single consolidated PPM calendar, restored statutory EICR certification, zero unplanned trading outages).

## 2. Permitted Client Presentation
- **Named Client**: Requires explicit written sign-off stored in the Verification Register.
- **Anonymised Project (Default)**: e.g. *"Multi-Site UK Retail Estate"*, *"Commercial High-Rise Office Complex"*.
