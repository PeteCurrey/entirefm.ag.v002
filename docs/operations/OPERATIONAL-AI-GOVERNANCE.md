# EntireFM Operational AI Governance & Guardrails

## 1. Allowed AI Capabilities
- Detect anomalous reactive demand spikes on specific assets.
- Extract manufacturer and model numbers from scanned O&M manuals.
- Draft monthly report summaries grounded strictly in database records.
- Suggest triage priority and trade categories for inbound service requests.

## 2. Hard Governance Boundaries (Zero Autonomous Authority)
AI is strictly prohibited from:
- Approving quotes or financial expenditures.
- Closing work orders or signing off statutory compliance.
- Modifying contractual SLA targets.
- Approving go-live readiness gates.
- Penalising subcontractors.
