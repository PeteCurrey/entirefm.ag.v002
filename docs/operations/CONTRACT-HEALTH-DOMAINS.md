# EntireFM Multi-Domain Contract Health Telemetry

## 1. Multi-Domain Health Breakdown
Contracts are never reduced to one single opaque percentage. Health is evaluated independently across 10 domains:

1. **Service Requests**: Inbound volume, triage speed, unassigned queue.
2. **SLA**: Real-time adherence across response, attendance, and resolution targets.
3. **PPM**: Delivery on-time percentage and SFG20 statutory completion.
4. **Compliance**: Valid certificate coverage and clear responsibility mapping.
5. **Defects & Remedials**: Open quotes, approved works, and remedial turnaround.
6. **Supply Chain**: Subcontractor acceptance rates and insurance validity.
7. **Client Actions**: Pending quote sign-offs, POs, and access permits.
8. **Costs & WIP**: Committed costs vs agreed contract budget baselines.
9. **Reporting**: Monthly governance packs delivered on schedule.
10. **Data Quality**: Broken lineage, missing asset tags, or incomplete location mapping.
