# EntireFM Guide Design Review & Architectural Comparison

## 1. Executive Summary
This design review audits and compares the initial four flagship evergreen guides against the EntireFM visual design standard (Porsche/Bloomberg elegance + modern technical engineering clarity):
1. **The Complete Guide to Facilities Management** (`/resources/guides/facilities-management-guide`)
2. **The Complete Guide to Planned Preventative Maintenance** (`/resources/guides/ppm-guide`)
3. **How to Build an FM Asset Register** (`/resources/guides/asset-register-guide`)
4. **The Facilities Management Tender Guide** (`/resources/guides/fm-tender-guide`)

---

## 2. Design System Components
Each guide leverages:
- **Restrained Editorial Theme**: Deep slate void backgrounds (`#0B0E14`), crisp white typography (`#FFFFFF`), subtle emerald accents (`#10B981`), and technical borders (`#1F2937`).
- **Interactive Sticky Reading Progress & Table of Contents**: Allows readers to jump directly between technical chapters on desktop, with collapsible drawer on mobile.
- **Dedicated Technical Diagrams & Data Tables**:
  - Service Delivery Comparison Matrix (Hard FM vs Soft FM vs Total FM)
  - Statutory vs Manufacturer vs Discretionary PPM Frequencies
  - ISO 55000 / Uniclass Asset Hierarchy Architecture
  - 10-Stage FM Procurement & Tender Evaluation Scoring Model
- **Non-Invasive Commercial Pathways**: In-context tool integrations (PPM Schedule Builder, FM Health Check, Tender Brief Generator) without disruptive popups or aggressive gated paywalls.
