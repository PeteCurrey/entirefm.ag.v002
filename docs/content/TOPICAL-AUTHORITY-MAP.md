# EntireFM Topical Authority Map

## 1. Structure of Topical Clusters
This authority map defines the relationship across commercial service pages, interactive tools, statutory compliance guides, glossary terms, and our new evergreen guides:

### Cluster A: Planned Preventative Maintenance (PPM)
- **Commercial Page**: `/ppm`
- **Flagship Evergreen Guide**: `/resources/guides/ppm-guide`
- **Interactive Tool**: `/tools/ppm-schedule-builder` & `/tools/ppm-estimator`
- **Glossary Terms**: PPM, Asset Register, Reactive Maintenance, SFG20, C1/C2 Defects
- **Compliance Hub**: `/compliance`

### Cluster B: FM Procurement & Tendering
- **Commercial Page**: `/services`
- **Flagship Evergreen Guide**: `/resources/guides/fm-tender-guide`
- **Interactive Tool**: `/tools/tender-brief` & `/tools/fm-roi-calculator`
- **Glossary Terms**: Total FM, Hard FM, Soft FM, SLA, KPI, Mobilisation

### Cluster C: Estate Engineering & Asset Registers
- **Commercial Page**: `/mechanical-electrical`
- **Flagship Evergreen Guide**: `/resources/guides/asset-register-guide`
- **Interactive Tool**: `/tools/fm-health-check`
- **Glossary Terms**: Lifecycle Costing, CAFM, Condition Survey, Asset Hierarchy

### Cluster D: Broad FM Industry & Educational Authority
- **Commercial Page**: `/`
- **Flagship Evergreen Guide**: `/resources/guides/facilities-management-guide`
- **Hub & Intelligence**: `/resources/guides`, `/fm-intelligence`, `/academy`, `/facilities-management-glossary`
