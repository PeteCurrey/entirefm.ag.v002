## RULES FOR CONTENT TEAM

1. No `TO_VERIFY` claim may appear as a stated fact in production content.
2. `TO_VERIFY` claims may appear in draft copy marked `[PENDING VERIFICATION]`.
3. All certifications require documentary evidence (certificate number, official listing URL) before being marked `VERIFIED`.
4. `DO_NOT_USE` claims must not appear in any published page regardless of context.
5. DO NOT fabricate customer names, contract values, or performance statistics.
6. Once a claim is verified, update this register with the evidence reference and change status to `VERIFIED`.
