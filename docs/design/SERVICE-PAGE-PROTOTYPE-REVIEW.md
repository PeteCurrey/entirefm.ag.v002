## 1. Executive Summary & Design Objective

The EntireFM service-page architecture has been completely redesigned from flat, generic "SEO text walls" into high-authority, visually rich commercial service experiences. 

### Core Architectural Principles:
1. **Preserve 100% SEO Integrity**: Zero URL changes, zero redirects of historic routes, zero content reduction, full server-rendered HTML for search engines, valid JSON-LD structured schemas, and self-canonicals.
2. **Distinct Visual Character per Service**: Rather than stamping a single card grid across every page, each service discipline receives custom-engineered storytelling modules reflecting its true operational nature.
3. **Photographic & Editorial Richness**: Authentic photography across plant rooms, switchgear, rooftop chillers, and industrial environments paired with clean typography and EntireFM brand pink accents.
4. **Interactive Technical Features**: Unique interactive architectural diagrams and workflows (Building Systems Map, Airflow Journey, 7-Stage PPM Cycle, Industrial Environments Gallery).
5. **High-Converting Commercial UX**: Specific commercial CTAs tailored to service intent, integrated phone lines (`020 4586 5422`), and embedded light-variant enquiry forms.

## 3. Evaluation Across Quality Benchmarks

### A. Visual Identity & Brand Consistency
- **Gradient Discipline**: Consistent use of EntireFM brand tokens (`#FF3E9D` → `#ED3899` → `#C026D3` gradient accents, hairline rules, glowing badges).
- **Dark Ground Hierarchy**: High-contrast dark sections (`#0B1220` graphite / carbon) with subtle 60-degree facet rules create depth without looking like generic SaaS templates.
- **Micro-interactions**: Clean hover states on interactive tabs, smooth focus rings on form inputs, and responsive image zoom transitions.

### B. Service Specificity & Truthful Presentation
- **Zero Generic Duplication**: Each page presents genuine technical terminology, specific standards (BS 7671, SFG20, F-Gas, TM44, COSHH, IPAF), and realistic asset registers.
- **Accreditation Rigor**: No fake claims, unverified response times, or fabricated client counts. All accreditations match verified business documentation.

### C. Technical SEO & Schema
- **Complete Server-Rendered HTML**: Every capability, asset type, and FAQ is present in the initial server-rendered DOM.
- **Structured Data**: JSON-LD `Service`, `BreadcrumbList`, and `FAQPage` schemas injected into the head.
- **Route Parity**: All historic Wix URLs remain preserved with 200 OK status.

### D. Mobile Responsiveness & Accessibility
- **Touch Targets**: All interactive tabs, buttons, and form inputs exceed 44px minimum tap targets.
- **Visual Contrast**: Tested against WCAG 2.1 AA standards for typography over photographic overlays.
- **Graceful Stacking**: Horizontal journeys and system maps automatically transform into clear vertical accordions and step cards on mobile viewports.
