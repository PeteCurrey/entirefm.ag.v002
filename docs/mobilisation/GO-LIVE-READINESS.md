# EntireFM Go-Live Readiness Review Specification

## 1. Hard Go-Live Readiness Gates
Before a contract can be switched to `LIVE`, the following blocking items must be verified:

1. **Contract Signed & Accepted**: Canonical `client_accounts` and `contracts` records live.
2. **Site Access & Keyholders Confirmed**: Addresses, security access, and hazard registers verified.
3. **Asset Baseline Ingested**: Mechanical, electrical, and HVAC assets loaded and mapped.
4. **Compliance Gaps Identified**: Statutory responsibilities assigned (EntireFM vs Client vs Landlord).
5. **PPM Schedule Approved**: SFG20 maintenance routines generated and approved.
6. **24/7 Helpdesk Routing Active**: Priority SLAs and emergency escalation paths configured.
7. **Delivery Coverage Confirmed**: Direct engineers or approved subcontractors assigned.

---

## 2. Non-Blocking Items
- Minor cosmetic asset serial numbers.
- Secondary client user portal training.
