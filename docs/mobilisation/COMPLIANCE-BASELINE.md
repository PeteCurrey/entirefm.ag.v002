# EntireFM Statutory Compliance Baseline Specification

## 1. Statutory Responsibility Segregation
Every compliance requirement must be clearly attributed to one responsible party:
- `ENTIREFM RESPONSIBILITY`
- `CLIENT RESPONSIBILITY`
- `LANDLORD RESPONSIBILITY`
- `OTHER SUPPLIER`
- `TO BE CONFIRMED`

## 2. Gap Register & Evidence Management
- Missing compliance certificates generate an internal `EVIDENCE_NOT_AVAILABLE` gap.
- No assumption of compliance without valid documentation.
