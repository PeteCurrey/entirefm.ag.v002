# EntireFM Sector Estate Strategy & Authority Model

## 1. Executive Summary
The EntireFM sector estate is built to serve real facilities management buyers (Facilities Managers, Estates Directors, Operations Managers, Procurement Heads) across UK commercial, industrial, and institutional property.

Each sector page moves beyond generic promises into specific operational reality:
- **Operating Context**: Footfall, hours, access windows, critical systems.
- **Operational Priorities**: Plant reliability, uptime, tenant comfort, statutory compliance.
- **Service Mappings**: Which services physically matter to this environment.
- **Buyer & Procurement Guidance**: Scope definition, asset data readiness, red flags, questions to ask an FM provider.
- **Credible Proof**: Verified anonymised case studies and contractor network trade standards.

