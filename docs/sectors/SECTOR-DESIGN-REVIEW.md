## 3. Differentiation Verification
- **Unique Narratives**: Zero boilerplate phrases ("unique challenges", "tailored solutions", "seamless operations").
- **Real Engineering Scopes**: Exact physical systems detailed (dock levellers, busbars, AHUs, chilled water loops, EICRs, DW144/DW145 ductwork).
- **Distinct Buyer Journeys**: Every sector page links to relevant interactive tools (`/tools/tender-brief`, `/tools/ppm-schedule-builder`) and evergreen guides.
- **Proof Integrity**: Only verified anonymised engineering case studies and contractor network trade standards are rendered.
