# EntireFM CTA Design System & Hierarchy

## 1. Coherent CTA Hierarchy
1. **Primary Commercial Action**: High-intent, specific proposal or survey requests.
   - *Example*: `"Discuss Your PPM Maintenance Programme"`, `"Request a Site Survey"`.
2. **Secondary Educational / Planning Action**: Low-friction next step for research/planning stage.
   - *Example*: `"Launch PPM Schedule Builder"`, `"Read the FM Tender Guide"`.
3. **Tertiary Distribution Action**: Non-intrusive newsletter subscription at the footer of articles.
   - *Example*: `"Join the FM Briefing"`.

## 2. Strictly Prohibited CTA Patterns
- No generic SaaS hype (`"Transform Your Business Now"`, `"Unlock Instant ROI"`).
- No deceptive entry popups, full-screen takeover modals, or fake countdown timers.
- No CTA clutter (max 2 distinct action tiers per section).
