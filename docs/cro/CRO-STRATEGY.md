# EntireFM Content-Led Conversion & CRO Strategy

## 1. Executive Summary
The EntireFM conversion model moves prospective commercial clients naturally through a structured buying journey without aggressive popups, synthetic urgency, or interrupting editorial guidance.

```
  DISCOVERY ──► LEARNING ──► PLANNING ──► COMPARING ──► BUYING
  (Blog/Search)   (Guides/Glossary) (Tools/Calculators) (Services/Sectors) (Enquiry/Phone)
```

---

## 2. Intent Stages & Page Mappings
1. **DISCOVERY (Blog & Informational Insights)**: Focus on topical education and natural onward navigation to guides or the newsletter.
2. **LEARNING (Flagship Evergreen Guides & Compliance)**: In-depth technical instruction with contextual links to interactive tools and related services.
3. **PLANNING (Interactive FM Tools & Calculators)**: Free ungated utility providing actionable outputs (PPM Schedules, Health Checks, Tender Briefs) with clear next-step commercial options.
4. **COMPARING (Commercial Services & Sector Pages)**: Clear scopes, SLA governance, engineering standards, and specific commercial CTAs.
5. **BUYING (Contact & Proposal Request Hub)**: Low-friction enquiry pathways with automatic context preservation (service, location, source page).
