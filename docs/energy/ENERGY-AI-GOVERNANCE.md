# EntireFM Energy AI Governance & Control Boundaries

## 1. Allowed Energy AI Capabilities
- Detect statistically anomalous overnight baseload demand.
- Flag out-of-hours equipment runtime against configured building operating schedules.
- Draft plain-English client performance commentary referencing verified meter rows.

## 2. Hard Governance Boundaries (Zero Autonomous Authority)
AI is strictly prohibited from:
- Writing or altering BMS setpoints, schedules, or plant control logic.
- Approving energy efficiency capital works or expenditure.
- Guaranteeing financial savings to clients.
- Declaring carbon neutrality or ESG compliance without accredited third-party verification.
