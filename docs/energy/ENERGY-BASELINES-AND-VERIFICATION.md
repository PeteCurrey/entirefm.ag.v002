# EntireFM Energy Baselines & IPMVP-Style Verification (M&V)

## 1. Baseline Governance
- Project baselines must be established across a verified representative period (e.g. 12 months pre-intervention).
- Baselines cannot be modified post-implementation without logged change reasons and audit approval.

## 2. Savings Classification
- **`ESTIMATED`**: Pre-implementation business case projection.
- **`VERIFIED`**: Post-intervention measurement period completed with normalised comparison against approved baseline.
- **`INCONCLUSIVE`**: Data gaps or substantial occupancy changes prevent mathematical verification.
