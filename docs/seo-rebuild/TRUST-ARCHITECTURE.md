| Body / Accreditation | Relevance & Standards Governed | Visual Placement |
|---|---|---|
| **SafeContractor Approved** | Health & Safety competence across all Hard & Soft FM delivery | Global Trust Strip, Footer, M&E & Cleaning Pages |

| **Gas Safe Register** | Mandatory legal certification for all commercial boiler, heating & gas works | `/plumbing-gas`, `/mechanical-electrical`, `/hvac-contractor` |
| **NICEIC Approved Contractor** | Commercial electrical safety, periodic inspection & testing (EICR) | `/mechanical-electrical`, Hard FM Pages |
