## 2. Historic Traffic & Commercial Architecture Archetypes

Based on forensic crawls of **Wix Generation 1 (`efm-new`)** and **Wix Generation 2 (`efmsut17724`)**, commercial search traffic was historically driven by three primary clusters:

### Cluster A: High-Intent Hard FM & M&E Services
* `/mechanical-electrical` — Primary commercial traffic driver for bundled M&E engineering.
* `/hvac-contractor` — Targeted high-intent commercial HVAC replacement, servicing, and F-Gas compliance.
* `/ppm` — Captured planned maintenance procurement searches from property managers.
* `/plumbing-gas` — Captured commercial boiler plant and gas safety testing queries.
* `/fire-emergency-systems` — Captured life-safety and emergency lighting compliance searches.

### Cluster B: Regional City Hubs (London Dominance)
* **London 3-Page Estate:**
  * `/fm-london` — High-urgency, 24/7 helpdesk and rapid mobile engineer dispatch.
  * `/facilities-management-london` — Planned preventative maintenance (PPM) and total FM contract procurement.
  * `/london-facilities-management` — Corporate real estate, commercial managing agents, and multi-site portfolio governance.
* **Key Regional Operating Hubs:**
  * Manchester (`/facilities-management-manchester`, `/fm-manchester`, `/manchester-office-cleaning`)
  * Birmingham (`/facilities-management-birmingham`, `/fm-birmingham`, `/birmingham-facilities-management`)
  * Sheffield (`/facilities-management-sheffield`, `/fm-sheffield`, `/sheffield-facilities-management`)
  * Leeds (`/facilities-management-leeds`, `/fm-leeds`, `/leeds-facilities-management`)
  * Lincoln (`/facilities-management-lincoln`, `/fm-lincoln`, `/commercial-fm-lincoln`, etc.)

### Cluster C: Specialist Industrial & Commercial Cleaning
* `/industrial-cleaning` — High-value factory shutdown deep cleans, high-level access, and warehouse degreasing.
* `/cleaning-services` — Daily office contract cleaning and corporate hygiene.
* Regional specialist cleaning combinations (e.g. `/industrial-cleaning-london`, `/industrial-cleaning-sheffield`, `/commercial-cleaning-birmingham`).
