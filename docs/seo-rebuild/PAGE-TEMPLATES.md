## Template 3: Secondary Operational Location Page (e.g., `/fm-london`, `/fm-sheffield`)

* **Focus:** Rapid response, emergency engineering, reactive dispatch, immediate contact.
* **Layout:**
  1. High-impact emergency response hero with 2-hour SLA badge.
  2. Click-to-call direct helpdesk banner.
  3. Mobile engineering dispatch capabilities (HVAC, electrical, plumbing, fabric).
  4. 24/7 call-out protocols and CAFM ticket tracking.
  5. Fast 3-field emergency dispatch request form.

---

## Template 4: Programmatic Service × Location Page (e.g., `/industrial-cleaning/nottingham`)

* **Focus:** Highly specific commercial service intent tied to a geographic market.
* **Layout:**
  1. Service + City H1 (`Industrial Cleaning Services Nottingham`).
  2. Local industrial sector context (manufacturing, logistics, warehouse parks in the area).
  3. Specialized machinery and cleaning techniques used (scrubber-driers, high-level access).
  4. Local regional case study or client proof.
  5. Instant cleaning quote calculator / site survey request.

---

## Template 5: Commercial Sector Page (e.g., `/industrial-facilities-management`)

* **Focus:** Vertical operational problem-solving and procurement alignment.
* **Layout:**
  1. Sector-specific H1 (`Industrial & Manufacturing Facilities Management`).
  2. The 4 major pain points facing estate managers in this sector.
  3. Tailored Hard & Soft FM service packages for this sector.
  4. Compliance and health & safety mandates addressed (ATEX, COSHH, LOLER, DSEAR).
  5. Sector-specific case study with quantified results.
  6. Sector Audit Request Form.
