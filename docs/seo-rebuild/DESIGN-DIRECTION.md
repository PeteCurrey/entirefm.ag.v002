# EntireFM Design Direction & Visual Positioning Brief

> **Document:** `/docs/seo-rebuild/DESIGN-DIRECTION.md`  
> **Phase:** 02M — Visual Identity, Industrial Authority & UI/UX Standards  
> **Fundamental Principle:** **Combine the commercial clarity, obvious service navigation, and tangible operational credibility of the successful Wix site with modern, high-performance Antigravity Next.js execution.**

---

## 1. Brand Personality & Visual Positioning

EntireFM must look and feel like an **established, highly technical, national facilities management contractor** capable of maintaining complex, high-value commercial and industrial estates.

```
       ESTABLISHED & EXPERIENCED (Est. 2009)
                        ▲
                        │
   TECHNICAL &          │          NATIONAL &
   ENGINEERING-LED ◄────┼────►     OPERATIONALLY TANGIBLE
                        │
                        ▼
       MODERN, EFFICIENT & COMPLIANCE-DRIVEN
```

### Visual Tone Attributes:
* **Industrial Authority:** Precision engineering, clean plant rooms, heavy infrastructure.
* **Commercial Credibility:** Professional, established, compliant, transparent.
* **Modern Performance:** Fast-loading, crisp typography, clean micro-interactions, responsive clarity.

---

## 2. What to Embrace vs. What to Avoid

| ✅ Mandatory Design Standards | ❌ Anti-Patterns to Strictly Avoid |
|---|---|
| **Real FM Environments:** Photographs of commercial plant rooms, HVAC manifolds, electrical switchgear, liveried engineering vans, and liveried technicians. | **Generic AI / Fake Visuals:** Uncanny valley "robo-engineers", cartoonish illustrations, stock models in pristine hardhats holding empty clipboards. |
| **Obvious Commercial Navigation:** Clear, visible mega-menus categorizing Hard FM, Soft FM, Sectors, and Key Geographic Hubs. | **Obtuse "SaaS" Minimalism:** Hiding core services inside abstract dropdowns or cute 1-word marketing labels. |
| **High Information Density & Contrast:** Strong semantic hierarchy, readable tables, structured data callouts, clear bullet lists. | **Overdone Glassmorphism / Low Contrast:** Light gray text on white backgrounds, fuzzy translucent overlays that obscure technical copy. |
| **Purposeful Micro-Interactions:** Smooth accordion FAQs, sticky desktop contact strips, interactive calculators with clear results. | **Distracting Animation Gimmicks:** Floating 3D shapes, parallax scroll-jacking that delays access to commercial contact details. |
| **Visible Geography:** Prominent UK map, direct city phone numbers, clear local response time badges. | **"Nowhere & Everywhere" Vagueness:** Hiding company address or pretending to be a pure software company. |

## 3. Design System Foundations

### A. Color Palette (Engineering & Industrial Authority)
* **Primary Deep Navy / Slate (`#0B1325` / `#111827`):** Represents structural stability, corporate governance, and technical authority.
* **Safety / Engineering Amber Accent (`#F59E0B` / `#D97706`):** Used strictly for high-conversion CTAs, emergency dispatch callouts, and compliance alerts.
* **Clean Industrial White / Cool Gray (`#F8FAFC` / `#E2E8F0`):** Clean background contrasts that evoke surgical facility cleanliness.
* **Compliance Forest Green (`#059669`):** Accreditations, verified pass rates, and statutory guarantee badges.

### B. Typography
* **Primary Heading Font:** Clean, modern, technical sans-serif (e.g., `Inter`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`, or `Geist Sans`) with bold weights for H1/H2 hierarchy.
* **Body Font:** High-legibility neutral sans-serif with generous line-height (`1.6`) optimized for scanning technical specifications.

### C. Imagery Guidelines
* **Plant & Machinery:** Close-ups of commercial pumps, chillers, electrical boards, and ductwork.
* **Human Delivery:** Real engineers actively testing, maintaining, and auditing equipment.
* **Estate Scale:** Drone-perspective aerial views of industrial logistics hubs, corporate glass towers, and shopping centers.
