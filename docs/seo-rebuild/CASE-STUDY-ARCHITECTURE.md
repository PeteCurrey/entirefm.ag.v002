## 2. Core Case Study Matrix (Templates & Live Scenarios)

### Case Study 1: Multi-Site London Commercial Office Portfolio
* **Client Category:** Institutional Property Fund / Managing Agent
* **Geography:** London (Canary Wharf & City Core)
* **Services Delivered:** Hard FM, SFG20 PPM, HVAC Chiller Overhauls, 24/7 Reactive Helpdesk.
* **Sector:** Commercial Offices (`/commercial-facilities-management`)
* **Operational Challenge:** Fragmented subcontractor base causing delayed SLA responses and inconsistent statutory compliance tracking across 12 Grade-A office blocks.
* **EntireFM Solution:** Consolidated Total Hard FM under single contract, deployed `EntireCAFM` for real-time asset QR tagging, instituted mobile engineering vans with 2-hour Central London SLA.
* **Measurable Outcome:** 100% statutory compliance pass rate across all sites; 28% reduction in reactive maintenance call-out spend within 12 months.
* **Internal Linking Graph:**
  * Inbound from: `/facilities-management-london`, `/london-facilities-management`, `/mechanical-electrical`, `/ppm`, `/commercial-facilities-management`.
  * Outbound to: Same pages with contextual anchor text.

### Case Study 3: Manufacturing Plant & Factory Heavy Infrastructure
* **Client Category:** Heavy Precision Engineering & Manufacturing Facility

## 3. Case Study Standard Presentation Schema

Every case study page (and embedded summary block) must follow this 7-part anatomy:
1. **Headline & Meta Metadata:** Client Sector, Location, Service Scope, Contract Duration.
2. **The Estate / Facility Profile:** Square footage, asset count, operating hours.
3. **The Core Operational Challenge:** Compliance risk, cost leakage, poor responsiveness from previous contractor.
4. **The EntireFM Technical Solution:** Engineering standards applied (SFG20, Gas Safe, NICEIC), technology utilized (`EntireCAFM`).
5. **Verified Results & KPIs:** Concrete metrics (SLA attainment, cost savings, compliance %).
6. **Client Quote / Testimonial:** Verified feedback from Operations Director / Property Manager.
7. **Direct Conversion CTA:** "Request a Similar Site Survey for Your Facility".
