# EntireFM Research Methodology & Data Integrity Standards

## 1. Zero Synthetic Statistics
- No headlines or PR pitches may use invented survey percentages (e.g. "67% of FM directors...").
- Only verified public datasets (ONS, DESNZ, HSE, UK Government Property Agency) or genuine EntireFM surveys with documented sample sizes, methodologies, and limitations are used.

## 2. Mandatory Research Disclosures
Every published research piece or data story must disclose:
- Sample size ($N$) and participant criteria.
- Survey execution window and collection method.
- Complete question wording.
- Methodological limitations and sample confidence intervals.
