# EntireFM Media Target Model & Categorisation

## 1. Editorial Categories
1. **FM Industry Press**: FMJ, Facilitate Magazine, Premises & Facilities Management (PFM), TwinFM.
2. **Commercial Property & Real Estate**: Estates Gazette (EG), Property Week, React News, Place North West/Yorkshire.
3. **Building Engineering & Construction**: CIBSE Journal, Building Magazine, Construction News, RAC Magazine (HVAC/Refrigeration).
4. **Energy & Decarbonisation**: Energy in Buildings & Industry (EiBI), The Energyst.
5. **Business & Regional Media**: Yorkshire Post, Manchester Evening News Business, The Business Desk.
6. **Technology & Enterprise AI**: TechRound, UK Tech News, Enterprise AI publications.

## 2. Media Target Data Schema
- `publication_name`: Official title.
- `website`: Canonical domain.
- `category`: FM_PRESS | PROPERTY | BUILDING_ENGINEERING | ENERGY | TECH_AI | REGIONAL_BUSINESS.
- `editorial_focus`: Specific beats (e.g. decarbonisation, FM procurement, HVAC tech).
- `relationship_status`: UNCONTACTED | PITCHED | RESPONDED | COVERAGE_EARNED | DO_NOT_CONTACT.
