# EntireFM Digital PR Attribution & SEO Correlation

## 1. Multi-Dimensional Authority Metrics
We reject arbitrary single metrics such as "PR Value" or "Advertising Value Equivalent (AVE)". We measure:
- **Earned Editorial Backlinks**: Verified dofollow and nofollow links from reputable publications.
- **Brand Mentions & Citations**: Unlinked and linked mentions in industry press.
- **Direct Referral Traffic & Engagement**: Sessions and time on page from media coverage.
- **Search Authority Correlation**: Organic keyword impression lift across linked landing pages.

## 2. Historic Wix Link Equity Safeguard
- Restored historic Wix URLs carrying existing external backlinks are permanently protected with `canonical: 'self'` to preserve accumulated domain authority.
