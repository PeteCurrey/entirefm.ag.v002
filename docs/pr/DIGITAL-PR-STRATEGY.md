# EntireFM Digital PR, Link Earning & Industry Authority Strategy

## 1. Executive Summary
The EntireFM Digital PR & Industry Authority engine turns our authoritative evergreen content, interactive FM tools, and research into high-quality backlinks, journalist citations, and industry recognition without spam, paid links, or mass outreach.

```
  Research & Data / Interactive Tools / Technical Evergreen Guides
                                │
                                ▼
  Editorial Content Promotion & Expert Commentary Pipeline
                                │
                                ▼
  Curated Media Targets (FM Press, Property, Construction, Tech, Regional)
                                │
                                ▼
  Human-Reviewed Tailored Pitches (Strictly 1 follow-up max)
                                │
                                ▼
  Earned Editorial Backlinks, Citations & Unlinked Mention Recovery
                                │
                                ▼
  Search Authority Lift, Referral Traffic & Commercial Discovery
```
