# EntireFM Lead Qualification Methodology

## 1. Multi-Dimensional Qualification Fit

Qualification does not use opaque AI scores. Commercial reviewers evaluate 5 clear dimensions:

1. **Service Capability Fit**: Is the requirement within EntireFM core delivery (PPM, M&E, HVAC, Total FM, Cleaning)?
2. **Geographic Coverage**: Is the site located within our national regional delivery network?
3. **Estate Scale**: Property type, asset criticality, single vs multi-site estate.
4. **Procurement Timeline**: Desired contract commencement date or tender deadline.
5. **Decision Authority**: Access to property director, estate manager, or commercial custodian.

---

## 2. Qualification Outcomes
- **UNREVIEWED**: New inbound submission.
- **QUALIFIED**: Meets commercial criteria; ready for Discovery.
- **POTENTIALLY_QUALIFIED**: Good fit but missing key details (requires follow-up call).
- **NOT_A_FIT**: Out of regional or technical scope.
- **SPAM / DUPLICATE**: Isolated from reporting.
