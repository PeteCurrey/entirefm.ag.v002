# Content Ownership & Search Intent Model

## 1. Intent Ownership Principles
Every indexable URL in the EntireFM estate maps to a designated primary query family and topic cluster.

## 2. Topic Clusters
1. `AI_TECHNOLOGY`: AI in Facilities Management, EntireCAFM, Predictive Analytics.
2. `PPM_MAINTENANCE`: Planned Preventative Maintenance, SFG20 Schedules, Asset Lifecycle.
3. `COMPLIANCE`: Statutory compliance, Building Safety Act 2022, Fire Safety Order, EICR, TMV.
4. `ME_ENGINEERING`: Mechanical & Electrical building services.
5. `HVAC`: Commercial chiller, heat pump, AHU maintenance & servicing.
6. `CLEANING`: Commercial cleaning, infection control, daily janitorial.
7. `FM_PROCUREMENT`: Tender evaluation, contract models (Total FM vs Hard FM), SLAs.
8. `FM_FUNDAMENTALS`: Facilities management glossary, duty of care, landlord obligations.
9. `SECTORS`: Logistics, Healthcare, Education, Heritage, Commercial Offices.
10. `LOCATIONS`: 21 Primary UK Regional FM Hubs.
11. `ENTIRECAFM`: Dedicated CAFM software, engineer mobile app, client portal.
