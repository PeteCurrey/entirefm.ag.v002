## 1. Cadence & Checklists

### Daily Monitoring (Days 1–14)
* **Search Console Crawl Stats:** Monitor Pages > Indexed vs Not Indexed count.
* **404 / 5xx Error Alerts:** Review server edge logs daily for unexpected 404s or crawl bottlenecks.
* **Form & Lead Telemetry:** Daily reconciliation of website leads against CRM/inbox delivery.
* **Phone Click Tracking:** Monitor daily `phone_click` events across London and regional hubs.
* **Sitemap Status:** Confirm 0 errors reported on all 11 sub-sitemaps in GSC.

### Weekly Monitoring (Weeks 3–8)
* **Keyword Rank Tracking:** Monitor positioning for Tier-1 keywords (`facilities management london`, `mechanical and electrical contractor`, `ppm maintenance`, `industrial cleaning`).
* **Organic Impressions & Clicks:** Compare weekly organic impression velocity against historic Wix benchmarks.
* **Indexation Parity:** Ensure 100% of the 229 submitted routes reach "Indexed" status.
* **Lead Conversion Rates:** Calculate conversion rate by landing page type (Core vs Location vs Sector).

### Monthly Strategic Review (Months 3–6)
* **Competitor Benchmark:** Re-run SERP audits against Oltec FM, Avero FM, and national FM competitors.
* **Historic Traffic Recovery Scorecard:** Measure % recovery of pre-migration traffic volumes.
* **New Growth Route Performance:** Evaluate initial ranking and lead generation from new commercial routes.
